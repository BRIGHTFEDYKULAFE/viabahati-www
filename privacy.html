Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 September 2026

This policy explains how Via Bahati handles personal information collected through this website, www.viabahati.app. Via Bahati is operated by Vestige Logistics Gh Ltd, Accra, Ghana.

Data that customers process inside the Via Bahati platform is governed by their customer agreement, not by this policy.

What we collect

  • Details you send through the contact form: your name, organisation, email address, enquiry type and message.
  • Emails you send to hello@viabahati.app.
  • Standard technical information recorded by our hosting provider when you load the site, such as IP address and browser type.

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, arrange demonstrations and continue a conversation you have started with us. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Services that handle your data

  • Web3Forms delivers contact-form submissions to our inbox.
  • Zoho Mail hosts our email.
  • Vercel hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's typefaces, which means your browser connects to Google's servers.

Some of these providers process data outside Ghana.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to respond and to maintain any resulting business relationship. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your rights

Under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), you can ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email hello@viabahati.app and we will respond promptly. You may also raise a concern with the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
