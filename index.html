The Challenge

Logistics powers economies—
but much of its intelligence is still hidden

Every day, thousands of shipments travel between ports, warehouses, factories, distribution centres, and retail locations across Africa. These movements generate valuable operational information.

Yet that information is often fragmented across spreadsheets, messaging apps, paper documents, and disconnected software systems.

The challenge is not a lack of data. The challenge is turning data into intelligence that organisations can trust and act on.

  • Delays are discovered after they happen
  • Financial institutions assess businesses with limited operational visibility
  • Insurance decisions rely on incomplete risk information
  • Customers lack real-time transparency into their shipments
  • Governments have no unified view of national logistics performance
Traditional logistics software tells you where shipments are.

Via Bahati explains what is happening, predicts what is likely to happen next, recommends the best course of action, and continuously learns from every operational outcome.

This transforms logistics from reactive operations into intelligent decision-making.

Frequently asked questions

Is our data isolated from other organisations? +

Yes. Via Bahati uses Row-Level Security (RLS) enforced at the database layer — not just application code. Your organisation's data is completely isolated from all other tenants. No other company can see your shipments, routes, drivers, or operational data under any circumstances.

Which countries does Via Bahati operate in? +

Via Bahati is currently live in Ghana, with all 12 national corridors mapped in Atlas™. West Africa regional expansion — covering Côte d'Ivoire, Togo, and the ECOWAS corridors — is planned for 2027. We are happy to discuss cross-border requirements during your discovery call.

How long does onboarding take? +

Most organisations are fully live within two weeks of their discovery call. This includes platform configuration, user accounts, driver WhatsApp pipeline setup, and a training session with your operations team.

What data do you need from us to get started? +

We need your active corridors, driver contact numbers (for WhatsApp check-ins), and basic shipment details. We do not require you to migrate any existing system — Via Bahati works alongside your current operations from day one.

Is there an API for integration with our existing systems? +

Yes. Via Bahati provides a REST API for shipment data, carrier scores, corridor risk, and GLI Index data. API access is available on the Professional and Enterprise plans. Contact us to discuss your integration requirements.

What does the Via Bahati Score™ mean for our financing? +

The Via Bahati Score™ is an independent operational trust rating built from your delivery performance, compliance record, route behaviour, and incident history. It provides banks and insurers with the operational evidence they need to make credit and underwriting decisions. A higher Score opens access to better financing terms and insurance rates.
